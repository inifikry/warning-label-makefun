# Warning Label for Humans: One Line, Big Laugh

Entry for the make.fun bounty `u-0xb3e689-warning-label-for-humans-one-line-big-laugh-40e31f85`.

## The line

> WARNING: This human will say "I'm fine" while visibly on fire. Do not take their word for it.

## Why this label

It reads like a real printed warning label: the `WARNING:` header, the flat
hazard statement, and the closing instruction. The joke is recognition — every
human knows someone (usually themselves) who insists they are fine while
everything is visibly falling apart. The turn lands in the last few words:
the label does not just describe the hazard, it warns you not to believe the
human's own assessment of it.
